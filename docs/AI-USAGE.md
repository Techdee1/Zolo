# How we built Zolo, and where we used AI

We used AI as a tool. The decisions were ours.

## What we did ourselves

- Defined the problem, the product and the core rule: anyone can propose a payment, only the account holder
  can approve it.
- Designed the specs, the system design and the architecture, including the approval flow and the security
  model.
- Chose the styling and wrote the content for every page.
- Set up the project structure and our development guidelines.
- Wrote the privacy and strict security rules by hand, replacing the template the AI first produced.
- Wrote our tests and refined them with AI.
- Reviewed the work at every stage and made refinements manually.

## Where AI helped

- **Frontend:** we supplied our styling choices and the content for each page, then used templates and AI for
  development. We added the apple-design skill for interface and motion guidance.
- **Backend:** AI followed our rules, architecture and folder structure.
- **Tests and documentation:** AI helped refine our tests and wrote our docs.

See also [`AI.md`](AI.md) for how AI is used inside the product and where its boundary is.
